About us

A workforce partner that grows with your business

Ebutsi Holdings and Ebutsi RSA form a privately owned labour outsourcing company with a proud history of providing workforce solutions tailored to our clients' changing needs.

Since entering the outsourced labour market in 1999, we have supplied skilled labour and tradesmen to key industries, including furniture manufacturing and logistics. In 2010 we added Stock Control and Risk Management services to help businesses reduce losses and improve profitability.

Today we serve 10 different industries and employ more than 2,000 staff across South Africa. Our clients include leading retail, manufacturing, warehousing and logistics companies.

We keep up with industry trends and changes in legislation so that our clients stay compliant, and we tailor our services to each business we work with.
